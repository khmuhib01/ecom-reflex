UrbanEats: Scaling a Cloud Kitchen Operation

How a delivery-only restaurant increased revenue by 220% while reducing operational costs

The Challenge

UrbanEats, a fast-growing cloud kitchen in Chicago, was struggling to manage their rapid expansion. Operating three virtual brands from a single kitchen, they faced:

  • Disjointed Systems

    Separate platforms for orders, inventory, and POS leading to data inconsistencies

  • Delivery Coordination

    Difficulty managing multiple delivery providers and driver assignments

  • Inventory Waste

    30% ingredient waste due to poor cross-brand utilization tracking

The Solution

We implemented an integrated NexaSuite solution combining our Food Delivery Platform with the mPOS system and Cloud Kitchen Management tools:

Implementation Timeline

Week 1-2: Discovery & Planning

Workflow analysis and integration requirements

Week 3-4: System Setup

Menu migration and staff training

Week 5-6: Delivery Integration

Connected 3 delivery providers to unified dashboard

Week 7-8: Go Live & Optimization

Full transition with performance monitoring

Unified Operations

Single platform for all three virtual brands

Smart Inventory

Cross-brand ingredient tracking

Delivery Optimization

Automated driver assignment

Real-time Analytics

Performance tracking by brand

Reduction in ingredient waste

Average delivery time (down from 22 min)
