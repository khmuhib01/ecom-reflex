Our eCommerce Development Process

Transparent, collaborative, and designed for your success

1. Discovery & Planning

1-2 weeks

We begin with an in-depth discovery phase to understand your business objectives, target audience, competitors, and technical requirements. This foundation ensures we build the right solution for your needs.

Key Activities:

  • Business requirements gathering
  • Competitor and market analysis
  • Technical assessment
  • Project roadmap creation
  • KPI definition and success metrics

Deliverables:

Project Plan Technical Specification Sitemap Wireframes

2. UI/UX Design

2-4 weeks

Our design team creates visually stunning, conversion-focused interfaces that reflect your brand identity while prioritizing user experience and accessibility.

Key Activities:

  • Brand alignment and mood boards
  • High-fidelity mockups creation
  • Mobile-first responsive design
  • User flow optimization
  • Interactive prototyping

Deliverables:

UI Design System Interactive Prototypes Style Guide Asset Library

3. Development

4-12 weeks

Our developers bring designs to life using best practices and modern technologies, with regular updates to keep you informed throughout the process.

Key Activities:

  • Frontend and backend development
  • Third-party integrations
  • CMS setup and configuration
  • Payment gateway implementation
  • Performance optimization
  • Bi-weekly progress demos

Deliverables:

Development Environment Staging Site API Documentation Admin Training

4. Quality Assurance

2-3 weeks

We conduct rigorous testing to ensure your store performs flawlessly across all devices, browsers, and use cases before launch.

Key Activities:

  • Functional testing
  • Cross-browser and device testing
  • Performance and load testing
  • Security audit
  • Accessibility compliance check
  • User acceptance testing (UAT)

Deliverables:

Test Reports Performance Metrics Bug Fix Log Security Certificate

5. Launch

1-2 weeks

We manage a smooth, stress-free launch with comprehensive planning to ensure zero disruption to your business operations.

Key Activities:

  • Final content population
  • DNS and hosting configuration
  • SSL certificate installation
  • Performance optimization
  • Soft launch and monitoring
  • Full production launch

Deliverables:

Launch Checklist Backup Plan Analytics Setup Post-Launch Report

6. Optimization & Growth

Ongoing

After launch, we continue to monitor, optimize, and enhance your store to drive continuous improvement and business growth.

Key Activities:

  • Performance monitoring
  • Conversion rate optimization
  • SEO enhancements
  • Security updates
  • Feature additions
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

Deliverables:

Monthly Reports Optimization Roadmap Support Tickets Feature Updates

Why Our Process Delivers Results

Our methodology is designed to minimize risk, maximize efficiency, and ensure your store performs at its best.

Structured Yet Flexible

While we follow a proven framework, we adapt our approach to your unique business needs and evolving market conditions.

Transparent Communication

Performance-Focused

Data-Driven Decisions
