B2B eCommerce vs. B2C: Key Differences

Understanding the unique requirements of business buyers.

Feature B2C eCommerce B2B eCommerce
Pricing Fixed retail pricing Tiered, negotiated, contract-based
Order Size Single items or small bundles Bulk quantities, pallet orders
Checkout Instant purchase Quote requests, approval workflows
Payment Credit card, PayPal Net terms, purchase orders, ACH
Catalog Standard public catalog Account-specific products/pricing
Users Individual shoppers Multiple roles with different permissions

B2B eCommerce FAQs

Answers to common questions about B2B eCommerce development.

B2B eCommerce requires fundamentally different features: account hierarchies with multiple users, complex pricing structures, quote management, bulk ordering, approval workflows, and deep ERP/CRM integrations. The user experience must accommodate professional buyers who need efficiency and access to specialized product data, not just attractive merchandising.

Our B2B clients typically see:

• 30-50% reduction in order processing costs
• 20-40% increase in order frequency
• 15-30% larger average order values
• 25-60% decrease in customer service inquiries
• 2-5x more products per order compared to phone/email orders

The exact ROI depends on your industry, product complexity, and current sales processes.

We implement sophisticated pricing engines that can handle: customer-specific contracts, volume discounts, seasonal promotions, geographic pricing, and even real-time price calculations based on current inventory levels or commodity prices. Prices can be pulled from your ERP or managed within the eCommerce platform with appropriate approval workflows.

Absolutely. We specialize in integrating eCommerce platforms with existing ERP, CRM, PIM, and other business systems. In fact, we recommend against replacing operational systems that are working well. Our integration approach maintains your ERP as the system of record while providing a modern digital commerce layer for your customers.

We take a phased approach: 1) Create comprehensive help documentation and video tutorials, 2) Train your internal sales team first so they can support customers, 3) Offer live training webinars for key accounts, 4) Implement in-app guidance and tooltips, 5) Provide dedicated onboarding for high-volume buyers. We also recommend running a pilot program with your most tech-savvy customers before full launch.

We design hybrid solutions where your sales team can place orders through the same system customers use, ensuring consistency whether orders come through the website, email, or phone. This gives you the flexibility to gradually transition customers to self-service while maintaining service for those who need it. Many clients implement incentives for customers to move online.
