Multi-Store Management FAQs

Answers to common questions about managing multiple eCommerce stores.

Our platform is designed to scale with your business:

• Basic plans support up to 5 stores
• Professional plans support up to 25 stores
• Enterprise solutions have no practical limit

We've successfully implemented solutions for retailers with 100+ stores worldwide. The system architecture ensures performance doesn't degrade as you add more stores.

Absolutely. Our system supports complete branding independence while maintaining centralized management:

• Unique designs/themes for each store
• Separate logos, color schemes, and styling
• Customized product presentation
• Localized content and messaging

You maintain full creative control over each store's look and feel while benefiting from unified operations.

Our intelligent inventory allocation system provides multiple configuration options:

• Shared Pool: All stores draw from the same inventory
• Dedicated Allocation: Reserve specific quantities for each store
• Regional Allocation: Assign inventory to geographic regions
• Hybrid Models: Combine approaches for different product lines

You can set minimum stock levels, automatic replenishment rules, and override allocations when needed. The system prevents overselling in real-time across all channels.

Yes, our system provides flexible pricing management:

• Set base prices that apply globally
• Create store-specific price overrides
• Implement regional pricing strategies
• Manage currency conversions automatically
• Schedule price changes in advance

You can update prices across all stores simultaneously or customize pricing for specific markets while maintaining centralized control.

Our reporting dashboard provides multiple views:

• Consolidated Reports: View all stores as one business
• Store-Level Reports: Analyze individual store performance
• Comparative Reports: Compare stores side-by-side
• Custom Groupings: Create reports for regions, brands, or categories

All reports can be scheduled, exported, and shared with team members based on their permissions. Real-time analytics help you spot trends and opportunities across your store network.

Our proven implementation process ensures smooth onboarding:

1. Discovery: Audit current stores and define requirements
2. Configuration: Set up your management hub and connect stores
3. Data Migration: Transfer products, customers, and historical data
4. Testing: Validate all integrations and workflows
5. Training: Teach your team the management system
6. Go-Live: Switch to centralized operations
7. Optimization: Refine based on real-world usage

Typical implementations take 4-8 weeks depending on store count and complexity.
