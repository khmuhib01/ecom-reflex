Platform Migration FAQs

Answers to common questions about eCommerce platform migration.

Migration timelines vary based on complexity, but here are general guidelines:

• Small stores (under 1,000 products): 4-8 weeks
• Medium stores (1,000-10,000 products): 8-12 weeks
• Large enterprises (10,000+ products): 3-6 months

Factors affecting timeline include data complexity, custom features, integration requirements, and the platforms involved. We provide a detailed project plan after our initial discovery phase.

When done correctly, platform migration should have minimal negative SEO impact. We implement comprehensive strategies to preserve your search rankings:

1. Maintain URL structures whenever possible
2. Implement 301 redirects for any changed URLs
3. Migrate all metadata (titles, descriptions, alt text)
4. Submit updated sitemaps to search engines
5. Monitor rankings and traffic post-launch

Our clients typically see rankings fully recover within 4-8 weeks, often with improvements due to better site architecture.

We thoroughly document all custom functionality during the discovery phase and develop a migration plan for each element:

• Custom features: We rebuild using native capabilities of the new platform or develop equivalent custom solutions
• Integrations: We reconnect with existing APIs or implement alternative solutions that provide the same functionality
• Workflows: We analyze and adapt business processes to work optimally on the new platform

Our goal is to match or improve upon your current capabilities, never downgrade.

We employ several strategies to minimize or eliminate downtime:

• Staged migrations: Transfer data in phases while keeping the old site running
• Maintenance mode: Brief read-only periods if absolutely necessary (typically <1 hour)
• DNS propagation: Careful timing of DNS changes to minimize disruption
• Order syncing: Dual-system operation during critical periods to prevent lost sales

For most migrations, we achieve true zero-downtime transitions where customers never see a "closed for maintenance" message.

Our migration service includes comprehensive post-launch support:

• 30-day warranty period: Fix any migration-related issues at no extra cost
• Performance monitoring: Track site speed and functionality for 60 days
• SEO tracking: Monitor search rankings and traffic recovery
• Training: Admin and content team training on the new platform
• Ongoing support: Optional maintenance plans for continued optimization

We're committed to your long-term success, not just the technical migration.

Migration costs depend on several factors:

• Platforms involved: Some transitions are more complex than others
• Data volume: Number of products, customers, and orders
• Custom features: Amount of unique functionality to recreate
• Integrations: Third-party systems that need reconnection

Typical project ranges:

• Basic migrations: $10,000-$25,000
• Medium complexity: $25,000-$75,000
• Enterprise migrations: $75,000+

We provide fixed-price quotes after a detailed discovery process to eliminate surprises.
