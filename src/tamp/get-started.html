Our Simple Onboarding Process

We've streamlined our process to get your project started quickly and efficiently.

1

Initial Consultation

Schedule a free 30-minute discovery call where we'll discuss your business goals, requirements, and expectations.

2

Project Questionnaire

Complete our detailed project brief to help us understand your technical needs, design preferences, and timeline.

3

Proposal & Agreement

We'll prepare a customized proposal with scope, timeline, and investment. Once approved, we'll finalize the contract.

4

Kickoff Meeting

Meet your dedicated project team, review timelines, and establish communication protocols for your project.

5

Project Initiation

We'll begin with discovery and planning, then move into design and development based on the approved roadmap.

6

Regular Updates

Receive weekly progress reports, participate in review sessions, and provide feedback throughout the process.
