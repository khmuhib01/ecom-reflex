Support & Maintenance FAQs

Answers to common questions about our ongoing support services.

Our monthly updates include:

• Security patches: All critical and important updates for your platform and plugins
• Compatibility updates: Ensuring all components work together smoothly
• Performance improvements: Optimizations based on latest best practices
• Backend maintenance: Database optimization, cache clearing, log rotation
• Documentation: Detailed report of all changes made

We test all updates in a staging environment before deploying to your live site to prevent issues.

Our response times vary by plan and issue severity:

• Critical issues (site down, checkout broken): 30 min - 1 hour
• High priority (major functionality impacted): 2-4 hours
• Medium priority (minor issues): 8-12 hours
• Low priority (general questions, enhancements): 24-48 hours

Enterprise plans receive faster response times across all priority levels. We provide SLA guarantees for each support tier.

Yes, our Professional and Enterprise plans include development hours:

• Professional: 2 hours/month for small fixes and tweaks
• Enterprise: 10 hours/month for more substantial changes

Development hours can be used for:

- Small design changes
- Adding new products/categories
- Basic functionality additions
- Integration adjustments
- Custom report setup

Hours roll over for up to 3 months if unused. Additional hours can be purchased as needed.

Our emergency support process:

1. 24/7 Hotline: Dedicated emergency number for critical issues
2. Immediate Triage: On-call engineer assesses the problem
3. Rapid Response: Begin work within 30 minutes for Enterprise clients
4. Continuous Updates: Regular communication until resolution
5. Post-Mortem: Analysis to prevent recurrence

After-hours emergencies are defined as:

- Complete site outage
- Checkout/payment system failure
- Security breach
- Critical data loss

Standard after-hours rates may apply for non-emergency requests.
