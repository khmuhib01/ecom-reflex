About the Role

As a Senior Product Designer at NexaSuite, you'll lead the design of our core product experiences, working closely with product managers and engineers to deliver intuitive, beautiful solutions that solve real business problems. You'll have the opportunity to shape the future of our platform while mentoring junior designers.

What You'll Do

  • Lead end-to-end design projects from discovery to implementation
  • Conduct user research and translate insights into design solutions
  • Create user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes
  • Collaborate with engineering to ensure high-quality implementation
  • Contribute to and evolve our design system
  • Mentor junior designers and promote design best practices

What We're Looking For

  • 5+ years of product design experience, preferably in SaaS or enterprise software
  • Strong portfolio showcasing your design process and outcomes
  • Expertise in Figma, Sketch, or similar design tools
  • Experience conducting user research and usability testing
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills
  • Bachelor's degree in Design, HCI, or related field (or equivalent experience)

Nice to Have

  • Experience designing for B2B products
  • Front-end development skills (HTML/CSS/JS)
  • Experience with design systems at scale
