eCommerce SEO FAQs

Answers to common questions about SEO for online stores.

eCommerce SEO follows a typical timeline:

• 1-3 months: Technical fixes and initial optimizations show impact
• 3-6 months: Content improvements begin ranking
• 6-12 months: Authority building delivers compounding results
• 12+ months: Dominant positions in competitive categories

Unlike paid ads, SEO is cumulative - results accelerate over time as your domain authority grows. We provide monthly reporting to track progress against benchmarks.

We employ multiple strategies to overcome duplicate content issues:

1. Rewrite product descriptions: Create unique, detailed content that outperforms manufacturer copy
2. Add supplemental content: Bullet points, comparison charts, usage guides
3. Implement canonical tags: When rewrites aren't feasible
4. Leverage schema markup: Help search engines understand your content is authoritative
5. Build supporting content: Category pages, buying guides, and blog content that contextualizes products

Our approach ensures each product page offers unique value that deserves ranking.

We specialize in scalable SEO strategies for large inventories:

• Prioritization framework: Focus on high-potential products first
• Automated optimization: Bulk updates for metadata, URLs, and structure
• Template-based rewrites: Unique descriptions at scale
• Dynamic content generation: Leverage product attributes for SEO
• Smart indexing: Control which pages search engines prioritize

For enterprise stores, we develop custom solutions to make SEO manageable across massive catalogs.

eCommerce SEO requires specialized tactics:

• Commercial intent focus: Targeting buyers rather than researchers
• Product page optimization: Unique challenges with thin content
• Category page strategy: Handling faceted navigation and filters
• Conversion integration: SEO that drives sales, not just traffic
• Seasonal fluctuations: Preparing for peak shopping periods
• Competition with Amazon: Strategies to outrank marketplace listings

Our team specializes in these product-specific SEO challenges.
